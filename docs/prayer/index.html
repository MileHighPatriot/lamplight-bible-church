Prayer

You don’t have to
carry it alone.

Whatever you're facing, we'd count it a privilege to pray for you. Every request is read by a pastor and prayed over by name. You can keep it private, and you never need to be part of Lamplight to ask.

Other ways

Rather pray with someone in person?

  • Sundays at 8:30am

    The prayer team meets in Room 204 before first service to pray over every request by name. Anyone is welcome to join them.

  • After every service

    Someone from the prayer team is at the front after both services. Come up and they'll pray with you right there.

  • In a crisis

    Call the church at (303) 555-0164. After hours, the message gives you the on-call pastor's number.

If you’re in danger or thinking about ending your life, please call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911. Then let us know. We want to walk with you.
