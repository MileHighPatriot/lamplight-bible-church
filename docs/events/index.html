Every week

The regular rhythm.
No sign-up needed.

These happen every week unless we say otherwise. Just show up.

  • Sunday9 & 10:45am

    Sunday Worship

    Main auditorium

    Worship, then verse-by-verse teaching. Kids classes for newborns through 5th grade.

  • Sunday9am

    Foundations Class

    Room 204

    A six-week class on what we believe and why. Great if you're new to church or new to Lamplight.

  • Tuesday12:05pm

    DTC Lunch Study

    Conference room, 2nd floor

    Bring your lunch. We read the coming Sunday's passage together and you're back at your desk by 1.

  • Wednesday7pm

    Midweek: Through the Bible

    Main auditorium

    We're working through the whole Bible, Genesis to Revelation, a chapter at a time. Streamed live.

  • Wednesday7pm

    Lamplight Students

    The Loft

    Grades 6–12. Worship, teaching, small groups, and far too much pizza.

  • Saturday7am

    Men's Study

    Fellowship hall

    Coffee, breakfast burritos, and an hour in the Word before the weekend gets going.
