Your first Sunday

No awkward moments.
Here’s exactly what happens.

Most people check us out online before they ever walk in. So here's the whole morning, start to finish, so nothing catches you off guard.

  1. Step 1

    Park in back

    Pull into the lot behind the building. The front row is saved for first-time guests.

  2. Step 2

    Check the kids in

    Stop at the kids desk inside the east doors. You'll get a matching tag, and only you can pick them up.

  3. Step 3

    Worship & the Word

    About 25 minutes of singing, then we open the Bible and teach through the next passage. 75 minutes, start to finish.

  4. Step 4

    Stay for coffee

    Swing by the welcome table after. We'd love to meet you, and there's a small gift with your name on it.

Tell us you’re coming and we’ll have someone meet you at the door and walk you to the kids rooms.

Where we meet

Right off I-25,
in the heart of the Tech Center.

We meet in a former office building in the Tech Center that we've called home since 2019. Plenty of parking, and a short walk from the light rail.

  • Free parking in the lot behind the building.
  • Orchard Station (E and R lines) is a 6-minute walk.
  • Step-free entrance, elevator, accessible restrooms, and hearing-loop receivers at the sound booth.
